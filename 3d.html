On Saying "Bet!"

When a friend asked whether I could build a 3D prototype for his group project, I had zero experience with 3D design or modelling–so naturally, I said yes.

I downloaded Blender and through relentless YouTube watching, I figured out how to manipulate basic geometry, smooth edges, and color them accordingly. The result was my first 3D design/model: a rainwater filter featuring a dynamic rain simulation.

That project went so well it led directly to my next request—a custom shovel design with specific structural modifications for another friend’s coursework.

That “impulsive” decision proved that I can pick up complex technical tools like Blender and deliver satisfying results. I strive to bring this same quick-learning mindset to future multimedia design or engineering projects!
